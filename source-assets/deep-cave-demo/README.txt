Beneath the Echo — standalone Three.js r164 cave exploration.
Open the deployed site. Choose viewpoints or Enter the passage. WASD/arrows walk, drag looks, F toggles light, E reads notes. Touch directional controls appear on mobile.
Procedural geometry inspired by the deep-cave reference, not a pixel-identical reconstruction. Ambient sound starts only on request.
Third-party: Three.js MIT license retained in module header. Rock and metal textures reused from existing project Poly Haven CC0 assets. Reference artwork generated with built-in image_gen.
No edits to the existing game.
