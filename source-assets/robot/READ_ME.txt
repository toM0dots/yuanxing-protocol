AUREL / EV03 — humanoid sci-fi android

The latest human-shaped design is EV03_Aurel_Humanoid.blend.
EV02_Vanguard.blend is the earlier bulky mech direction.

AUREL includes curved metal body panels, a shaped face, optical eyes,
mechanical neck, exposed joint actuators, and two five-finger hands.
The 16-bone rigid-body rig supports posing the torso, head, arms and legs.
Finger segments are modeled individually but are not independently rigged.
No walk, combat or facial animation has been authored for this new design.

Open the .blend in Blender. Cameras 01, 02 and 03 provide full-body,
face-detail and rear views. F12 renders the active camera with Cycles.
Select AUREL_RIG and enter Pose Mode to adjust major body regions.

The GLB includes only the robot and rig. Its metal materials are portable;
the Blender micro-surface procedural shading is not fully portable to glTF.
It has not been integrated into the online game or substituted into the map.
Original EV01 and the previous environment files remain preserved.
