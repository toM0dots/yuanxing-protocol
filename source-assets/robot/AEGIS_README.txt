EV-04 AEGIS — Gundam-style armored humanoid mecha

Latest requested design: EV04_Aegis.blend
Engine exchange file: EV04_Aegis.glb
Renders: Aegis_hero.png, Aegis_rear.png, Aegis_detail.png

White armor, cobalt breastplate, crimson waist/feet/shield, gold V-fin and
chest vents, emerald sensors, segmented skirt armor, and backpack saber hilts.
Includes a 15-bone rigid mechanical rig and a 90-frame idle animation.
Select AEGIS_RIG and use Pose Mode for major body regions.
The detailed left hand is modeled but fingers are not independently rigged.
Walk cycles, combat animations and game integration are not included yet.

The earlier EV02 Vanguard and EV03 Aurel are retained as separate alternatives.
The existing map and original EV01 remain unchanged.
