# Field Operations visual assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP for the site. These are artistic scene assets, not photographs used for training or scientific observations.

- `/workspace/sites/ai-academy/dist/assets/expedition.webp`: cinematic exploration robot, matched to the user's approved visual concept. Prompt: retain the same worn graphite/titanium robot with restrained orange panels, camera lenses and articulated joints; kneeling to collect a sample on a rocky exploration site; distant scientific outpost, atmospheric morning light; open dark left side for HTML; no text, controls, branding or weapons. Photoreal physical materials and weight.
- `/workspace/sites/ai-academy/dist/assets/terrain-sheet.webp`: four generated ground views in a 2×2 sheet: coarse dry gravel, loose golden sand, damp silty mud, rough bedrock incline. Prompt: realistic rover-scale downward photography, consistent neutral daylight, edge-to-edge textures, no labels, borders or UI. Used only as scenery for explicitly simulated measurements/outcomes.
- `/workspace/sites/ai-academy/dist/assets/operations-sheet.webp`: six cinematic environments in a 3×2 sheet. Prompt: rover wheel/sample arm; offshore wind turbines; industrial robotics lab; orbital Earth and observation satellite; autonomous rover in an obstacle testing space; gloved engineer working on robot electronics. Physically plausible materials, restrained orange/blue accents, no cartoon proportions, labels or interface overlays.

The original generated PNGs are retained in the conversation's scratch assets. Final site-consumed files reside in the Git-backed checkout above. Real CNN imagery is separately attributed in `dist/assets/eurosat/metadata.json` and `LICENSE-EuroSAT.txt`.
