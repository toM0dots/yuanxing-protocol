远星协议：回声之下 — Story artwork pack v1

Four standalone cinematic PNG illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
The original outputs are preserved without resizing or retouching.
Full generation prompts are recorded in prompts.json.
Titles, dimensions and suggested uses are recorded in manifest.json.
These are 2D story illustrations and visual references, not 3D models or playable scenes.
No live-game changes were made for this image request.
